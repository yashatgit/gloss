Attention Is All You Need

The dominant sequence transduction models are based on complex recurrent or convolutional neural networks. We propose a new simple network architecture, the Transformer, based solely on attention mechanisms.

Self-attention, sometimes called intra-attention, is an attention mechanism relating different positions of a single sequence in order to compute a representation.

Explain this.
Self-attention lets each position in a sequence look at every other position to build its representation — so the model can weigh how much each word matters to every other word, all in parallel.
