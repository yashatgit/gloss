Attention Is All You Need

The dominant sequence transduction models are based on complex recurrent or convolutional neural networks. We propose a new simple network architecture, the Transformer, based solely on attention mechanisms.

Self-attention1, sometimes called intra-attention, is an attention mechanism relating different positions of a single sequence in order to compute a representation of the sequence.

Note ¹on “self-attention”×
Explain this.
Self-attention lets every position in a sequence look at every other position at once — each word weighs how much the others matter to it, in parallel rather than step-by-step.
